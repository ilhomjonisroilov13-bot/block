🛡️ So'z Qalqoni — Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 22-noyabr · English version below

Qisqacha

So'z Qalqoni hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotni yig'maydi, yubormaydi va boshqalar bilan ulashmaydi. Ilova internetga ulanmaydi. Barcha ma'lumot faqat sizning telefoningizda saqlanadi.

Ilova nima qiladi

So'z Qalqoni — o'zini nazorat qilish vositasi. Siz o'zingiz bloklanadigan so'z va ismlar ro'yxatini tuzasiz. Brauzer, Google yoki YouTube'ning qidiruv maydoniga shu so'zlardan biri yozilsa, ilova ogohlantirish ko'rsatadi va yozilgan matnni tozalaydi.

Qulaylik xizmati (Accessibility) ruxsati

Qidiruv maydoniga yozilgan matnni aniqlash uchun ilova Android'ning Qulaylik xizmatidan foydalanadi. Bu ruxsat faqat siz roziligingizni bergandan va xizmatni telefon sozlamalarida o'zingiz yoqqandan keyin ishlaydi.

  • Nimani o'qiydi: faqat brauzerlar, Google va YouTube kabi qidiruv ilovalarida qidiruv maydoniga yozilgan matn va brauzerning manzil qatori. Parol maydonlari o'qilmaydi.
  • Nima uchun: yozilgan matn sizning ro'yxatingizdagi so'zga mos kelishini tekshirish va ogohlantirish ko'rsatish uchun. Boshqa maqsadda ishlatilmaydi.
  • Matn saqlanmaydi. Tekshiruv telefonning xotirasida bir lahzada bajariladi va natijasidan keyin o'chiriladi. Hech qayerga yuborilmaydi.
  • Ilova ogohlantirish oynasini ko'rsatish, mos kelgan matnni tozalash va siz tugmani bosganda orqaga yoki bosh ekranga qaytarishdan boshqa amal bajarmaydi.

Qanday ma'lumot saqlanadi

Quyidagilar faqat telefoningizning ichki xotirasida saqlanadi:

  • Elektron pochta manzilingiz va parolingizning shifrlangan (xesh) ko'rinishi, ilovaga kirish uchun.
  • O'zingiz kiritgan bloklanadigan so'zlar ro'yxati va sozlamalar.
  • To'xtatilgan urinishlar soni va toza kunlar hisobi.

Bu ma'lumotlar serverga yuborilmaydi. Ilovani o'chirsangiz yoki ilova ma'lumotlarini tozalasangiz, ular butunlay yo'qoladi.

Ulashish va reklama

Ilovada reklama, tahlil (analytics) yoki uchinchi tomon kutubxonalari yo'q. Ma'lumot hech kimga sotilmaydi va ulashilmaydi.

Sizning nazoratingiz

  • Rozilikni ilovaning Sozlamalar → Maxfiylik bo'limidan istalgan vaqt qaytarib olishingiz mumkin.
  • Xizmatni telefonning Sozlamalar → Qulaylik bo'limidan o'chirishingiz mumkin.
  • Ilovani o'chirsangiz, barcha ma'lumot o'chadi.

Bolalar

Ilova bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va ulardan ataylab ma'lumot yig'maydi.

O'zgarishlar va aloqa

So'z Qalqoni — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Summary

So'z Qalqoni does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet access. Everything stays on your device.

What the app does

So'z Qalqoni is a self-control tool. You create your own list of words and names to block. If one of them is typed into the search field of a browser, Google or YouTube, the app shows a warning and clears the typed text.

Accessibility Service permission

To detect text typed into search fields, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service. It works only after you give consent inside the app and enable the service yourself in system settings.

  • What it reads: only text typed into search fields of browsers and search apps (such as Google and YouTube) and the browser address bar. Password fields are not read.
  • Why: solely to check whether the text matches a word on your list and to show a warning.
  • Text is not stored. The check happens instantly in device memory and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The app performs no actions other than showing its warning screen, clearing the matching text, and going back or to the home screen when you tap the corresponding button.

Data stored on your device

  • Your email address and a hashed form of your password, for signing in to the app.
  • Your list of blocked words and your settings.
  • Counters of stopped attempts and clean days.

None of this is sent to a server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Sharing and ads

There are no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs. No data is sold or shared.

Your control

  • Withdraw consent anytime in Settings → Privacy inside the app.
  • Turn the service off in your phone's Settings → Accessibility.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact
